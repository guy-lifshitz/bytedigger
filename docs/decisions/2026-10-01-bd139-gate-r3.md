### NOTE A: the unlink path when the scratchpad cannot be resolved is unspecified
> engine_py/tests/test_bd82_fresh_sessions_thread_ctx.py:185:     _invoke_review_llm(types.SimpleNamespace(org_config={}), prev)
> engine_py/bytedigger_engine/workflows/phase_workflows_common.py:139:         raise ValueError("org_config.scratchpad_dir required for phase_5_implement")

A GREEN that calls `_resolve_scratchpad(ctx)` unguarded for the unlink breaks `test_f6` and `bd82 test_r7`. The
full-suite delta catches this, so it is not a wrong-GREEN-passes risk. The fix is to mirror the fail-safe at
`:1007-1022`: on an unresolvable scratchpad or an `OSError`, log and skip the unlink, and never fail the step.
Add one sentence to §2 op1, and add `_invoke_review_llm` to the §3 audit grep.
