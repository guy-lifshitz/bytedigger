1. **Lone-surrogate path**, e.g. a member `path: "\ud800/a.md"` (valid JSON, a str, no NUL). Item 6 accepts it. `os.path.exists` swallows the `UnicodeEncodeError`, so the member counts as missing and the engine gives `E_ORACLE_MUTATED mutated:removed`.
