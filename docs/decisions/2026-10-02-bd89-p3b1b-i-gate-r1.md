## Adversarial edges

Edges that no §3 AC covers:

1. **Fallback re-dispatch with a non-None cfg.** An agent-sdk hang falls back to `claude-subprocess` through `:2146`. No AC checks that the fallback call also gets `None`, or that `straggler_cfg_ignored` is emitted exactly once across both dispatches rather than once per dispatch. If GREEN rebinds `straggler_cfg = None` right after the emit, both hold. If GREEN only replaces the literal at `:2117`, the fallback still forwards the dict. That is harmless after op2 but breaks the op4 wording "to every backend" (F5).
2. **Falsy non-None cfg (`{}`).** The spec says "not `None`", so `{}` must emit. A GREEN that writes `if straggler_cfg:` would pass every AC and silently skip `{}`. This is a boundary decoy (F6).
3. **Emit ordering against early returns.** A non-None cfg combined with an unknown backend, a manifest failure or a watchdog-probe failure: the spec does not say whether `straggler_cfg_ignored` comes before or after these returns. It is observable, but no AC pins it. This is advisory only.
