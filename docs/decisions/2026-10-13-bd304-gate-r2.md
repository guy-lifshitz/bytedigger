## Adversarial edges (not covered by the section 3 AC table)

1. **A1: the AC8 If.test allowance is file-wide.** `allowed_ids` accepts `__file__` in the test of *any* `ast.If`, not only the one whose body holds the insert. The `guarded` check is likewise not tied to the insert. A driver could keep `__file__` in an unrelated `if`, or bind it through a side-effecting call inside an If.test (e.g. `if not setattr(sys.modules[__name__], "E", __file__): ...`). This is contrived and advisory. To tighten it, restrict the allowance to the `If` whose `body` contains `mutations[0]`.
2. **A2: non-Name derivations bypass the `__file__` scan.** Examples: `globals()["__file__"]`, `vars()["__file__"]`, `__spec__.origin`, `sys.argv[0]`. A second derivation built from these is not seen. The insert itself must still contain a real `__file__` Name, so the inserted path stays inline. Advisory.
3. **A3: sys.path mutations other than calls are not counted.** `sys.path += [...]`, `sys.path[:0] = [...]` and `sys.path.__setitem__` are not counted by `_sys_path_mutations`. With `__file__` in them they are still caught, as stray or smuggled. Without it (e.g. `sys.path += ["engine_py"]`, which is cwd-relative) they escape AC8's "the only sys.path mutation is one insert". Advisory: also flag `AugAssign` / `Assign` whose target unparses to `sys.path` or `sys.path[...]`.
4. Carried from round 1, still advisory: non-UTF-8 / directory `--spec` (F7, N3), an empty spec file, multi-finding output order, and the AC9 corpus walking untracked trees.
